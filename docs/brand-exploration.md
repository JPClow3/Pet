# Naming decision — Zelupi

Selected on 28 October 2026: **Zelupi**, pronounced **ze-LU-pi**.
The user selected the care-centered direction for a Brazil-first product with
room to expand internationally. Ninupi was explored and was not selected.

The active identity, visual system, domain plan, and compatibility requirements
are maintained in [brand-guidelines.md](brand-guidelines.md). The application
uses Zelupi; this document is the record of the naming decision.

Zelupi is a proposed invented name associated with Portuguese “zelo”. The chosen
public descriptor is “Cuidados e história do seu pet”. The main promise remains
“O cuidado de hoje. A história inteira do seu pet”.

The initial registry checks on 28 October returned no registration for
`zelupi.com.br` or `zelupi.app`; `zelupi.com` was registered. No domains or handles
have been purchased or reserved. See the current guidelines for sources and
launch steps.
